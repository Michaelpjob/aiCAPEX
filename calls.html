CapEx Flow Calls

Where the market may be over- or underrating public suppliers based on theoretical 2026 CapEx flow. Each card surfaces the share of the Sankey segment a company captures, its implied revenue growth from consensus, a Flow Coverage Ratio (FCR), and a Modeled Fair Value derived from the excess flow vs. consensus growth gap.

Not investment advice. Modeled Fair Value uses a same-multiple revenue bridge: Modeled FWD Revenue = Consensus FWD + (Theoretical Flow − Implied Growth) × κ, where κ ∈ [0.05, 0.50] is a per-ticker conversion factor encoding competition, customer concentration, share decay, and non-DC tailwinds. Same multiple is applied to the higher revenue line; no P/E expansion is modeled. Numbers are directional, not precise price targets. Author may hold positions in some names listed.
