Headline finding

At $848B in 2026, the Big 5 hyperscalers plus AI-natives (CoreWeave, xAI, Stargate) are spending roughly 2× 2025 and 5× 2023. About a third — ~$290B — flows to GPUs alone, making accelerator silicon a larger line item than land, shell, electrical, cooling and grid combined. HBM/DRAM tightness adds an estimated $25B to Microsoft's 2026 plan.

What's measured, what's estimated

● Reported 2026 CapEx guides from Q1 2026 / FY26 Q3 earnings (MSFT, AMZN, GOOGL, META, ORCL, CRWV).
● Validated estimate Spending waterfall %: author estimate, cross-checked against Dell'Oro Group (GPU ~33%, electrical+mechanical ~50% of construction) and SemiAnalysis.
● Author projection 2027–2029 spender CapEx and within-segment supplier shares. Top-tier shares are public (NVIDIA ~80% of GPU, SK Hynix 55%+ of HBM, Schneider/Vertiv tied in power); project-level awards are not.
